**Audio-Visual Large Language Model Based Reasoning for AI assistant for People with Visual Impairments (September 2026)**
An embodied AI reasoning framework can be further expanded to support people with visual impairments (Algamdi, 2026; Gong et al., 2026). The system determines what information should be communicated, how much detail is needed, and when it should be delivered based on the user’s individual needs, surrounding environment, and degree of visual impairment. Safety-critical information, such as nearby physical obstacles, road structures, or navigation cues, should be communicated as immediately and concisely as possible, while less critical information can be delivered in greater detail when additional processing time is acceptable. Although previous studies have addressed related assistive problems, how Physical AI assistance can be further personalized according to different degrees and characteristics of visual impairment remains underexplored. A key challenge is therefore to jointly optimize communication strategy, accuracy, information prioritization, and latency through harmonized multimodal reasoning, enabling Physical AI systems to provide more timely, context-aware, and personalized assistance in dynamic environments. <br>
